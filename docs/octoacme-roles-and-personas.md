# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA and Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, test planning, and release readiness. They work with developers, product managers, and stakeholders to confirm that features meet acceptance criteria and production readiness standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and quality gates for each release
- Create and maintain test plans, cases, and regression coverage
- Validate acceptance criteria and identify user-impacting defects
- Coordinate automated and manual QA activities
- Communicate quality risks and release blockers early

### Goals
- Reduce production defects and customer-impacting issues
- Improve test coverage and confidence in delivery quality
- Help teams ship features with clear evidence of readiness

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and daily standups
- Defect triage and release quality reviews
- QA sign-off and risk updates for stakeholders

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Developers to define testability and fix validation.
- Partners with Product Managers and Project Managers on acceptance criteria and release decisions.
- Escalates quality or risk issues to the PM when a release is blocked or a customer-impacting defect is identified.

---

## Sponsor / Stakeholder Representative

### Role Summary
Sponsors and stakeholder representatives provide business context, decision-making authority, and executive alignment. They ensure the project remains connected to business priorities and strategic goals.

### Responsibilities
- Approve scope, budget, and timeline trade-offs
- Define business objectives and success measures
- Review milestone progress and major risks
- Resolve escalated conflicts or decision bottlenecks
- Communicate project outcomes and impacts to leadership

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable value to the organization
- Maintain alignment between strategy and delivery
- Support timely decisions that reduce churn and delays

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and milestone reviews
- Escalation pathways for business decisions and dependencies
- Sponsor briefings and investment or prioritization discussions

### Interaction with existing roles
- Provides strategic direction to Product Managers and Project Managers.
- Reviews and validates outcome measures set by Product Managers.
- Relies on Project Managers for status reporting, and on Developers/Architects for technical trade-offs and delivery confidence.

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches help teams work effectively, remove delivery friction, and improve collaboration. They support continuous improvement and healthy team rituals without owning product direction or technical decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate ceremonies such as sprint planning, standups, and retrospectives
- Remove blockers and improve team process effectiveness
- Support healthy collaboration and transparent communication
- Coach teams on iterative delivery, alignment, and continuous learning
- Help surface risks, dependencies, and improvement opportunities

### Goals
- Improve team flow, predictability, and collaboration
- Reduce friction in planning and execution
- Create a sustainable, learning-oriented delivery environment

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint events
- Retrospective follow-ups and team process coaching
- Check-ins with Product Managers and Project Managers on blockers and delivery health

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Project Managers to improve team execution and planning health.
- Supports Product Managers by helping teams refine workflow and backlog clarity.
- Collaborates with Developers to keep delivery processes sustainable and focused on team effectiveness.

---

## Technical Architect / Tech Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define technical direction, guide design decisions, and ensure solutions remain scalable, secure, and maintainable. They support the delivery team by balancing technical quality with execution speed.

### Responsibilities
- Define architecture patterns and technical standards
- Review technical designs and implementation approaches
- Identify risk areas, integration constraints, and technical debt
- Mentor developers and support design decision quality
- Support release readiness and operational resilience planning

### Goals
- Build systems that are maintainable, testable, and scalable
- Reduce avoidable technical risk and complexity
- Enable delivery teams to move quickly without undermining quality

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Technical risk reviews and dependency planning
- Architecture guidance during sprint planning and implementation work

### Interaction with existing roles
- Partners with Developers and Product Managers to align technical feasibility with business goals.
- Provides technical risk and trade-off guidance to Project Managers during planning and execution.
- Works with QA and Security roles to ensure the system is reliable, compliant, and production-ready.

---

## Security & Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure projects follow required standards and reduce risk to users, the business, and the organization. They help teams build securely and support compliant delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements for the project
- Review designs, code, and release plans for risk exposure
- Coordinate security testing, scanning, and remediation efforts
- Advise on data privacy, access controls, and incident readiness
- Support incident response and post-incident learning

### Goals
- Protect users, systems, and business data
- Minimize legal, policy, and security risk
- Enable secure delivery without slowing the team unnecessarily

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews during planning and release preparation
- Incident notification and remediation coordination
- Guidance for compliance checkpoints and pre-release checks

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers and Technical Architects to reduce vulnerabilities and validate secure design choices.
- Supports Product Managers and Sponsors by assessing business and regulatory risk.
- Coordinates with Project Managers and QA leads to ensure release readiness includes security checkpoints.

- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These role definitions help teams clarify accountability, escalation paths, and handoffs across delivery, governance, and quality.
